A Japan-based sourcing desk for global buyers.

FHM works with Japanese producers, manufacturers, processors and wholesalers to source products based on buyer requirements. The buyer does not need to identify the supplier first.

FHM receives the product requirement, target price, volume, destination, certifications, packaging and commercial conditions, then identifies suitable supply options in Japan.

  1. Japanese supply Producers, manufacturers, processors, wholesalers
  2. FHM Japan sourcing desk Sourcing, commercial coordination, trade execution
  3. Global buyers Importers, distributors, manufacturers, foodservice

More than sourcing.

FHM is a trading and coordination partner. Production, logistics and certification are carried out by the relevant suppliers and service providers.

  1. 01

    Supplier sourcing

    Identify and shortlist Japanese suppliers against your specification.

  2. 02

    Commercial coordination

    Quotations, samples, terms and order follow-up with the supplier.

  3. 03

    Export & logistics coordination

    Coordinate export preparation and shipment with the supplier and forwarders.

  4. 04

    Market & regulatory support

    Check destination requirements and request available documents before quotation.
