Your trading partner in Japan.

Fukuda Hotel Management LLC operates an international trading division connecting businesses with sourcing and commercial opportunities across markets.

Based in Tokyo, we facilitate cross-border trade through product sourcing, commercial coordination and cooperation with relevant manufacturers, suppliers and logistics providers.

Our approach is driven by business requirements, commercial feasibility and long-term trading relationships.

Trading across industries.

We explore sourcing and trading opportunities across multiple product categories, based on buyer requirements and available supply partnerships.

Food & Beverage

Japanese food products, ingredients, beverages and other wholesale food-related opportunities.

Electronics & Appliances

Consumer electronics, home appliances, cooling equipment and related commercial products.

Consumer Goods

Lifestyle products, everyday merchandise and other consumer-oriented goods.

General Trading

Additional commercial products and cross-border sourcing opportunities, evaluated project by project.

Category availability depends on each requirement and on the supply partners involved. We do not hold stock or publish a product catalogue.

Trade beyond borders.

From identifying commercial opportunities to coordinating transactions, we support businesses navigating international sourcing and cross-border trade.

  • Import & Export

    Commercial coordination for international shipments and cross-border trading opportunities, in both directions.

  • Global Sourcing

    Identifying suitable manufacturers, wholesalers and supply partners based on commercial requirements.

  • Wholesale Trading

    Connecting B2B buyers with commercial supply opportunities across product categories.

  • Trade Coordination

    Coordinating quotations, documentation and shipment preparation with relevant commercial partners.

Freight forwarding, customs clearance and certifications are handled by qualified external providers. Import eligibility and regulatory requirements are confirmed case by case.

From opportunity to execution.

The same four stages apply whether goods are leaving Japan or arriving in it. Samples and compliance checks are added when a transaction needs them.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Understand the product, destination, quantities and commercial objectives.

  2. 02

    Sourcing

    Identify potential supply options and suitable commercial partners.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    Evaluate commercial feasibility, pricing, terms and applicable requirements.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Coordinate confirmed orders and shipment-related arrangements with the appropriate partners.
